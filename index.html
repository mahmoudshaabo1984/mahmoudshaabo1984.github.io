About Me

Background & Education

I hold a BA in English Literature from the University of Aleppo (2011). I am currently based in the Sheffield/Doncaster region, UK. I enjoy merging my passion for languages, translation, and structured logic with writing clean code.

Vision & Career Goals

Being visually impaired, I have a first-hand understanding of the importance of web accessibility. My goal is to become a software engineer with a strong focus on backend systems, creating web applications that are inclusive and usable by everyone.

Development Environment

I primary work on Windows 11 with WSL2 (running Ubuntu 24.04). I rely on the NVDA screen reader as my primary tool for development, reading, and testing websites.
